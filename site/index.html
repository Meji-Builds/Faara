How a payment happens

Two phones, one hum, done.

  1. Ada owes Mama Tolu ₦5,000 for jollof rice.
  2. They hold their phones close. No account number, no bank name.
  3. Ada’s phone hums. A short sound and Bluetooth that only nearby phones pick up.
  4. Both phones show the same shapes, so Ada knows she’s paying the right person. She approves with her fingerprint.
  5. Done. The money lands instantly, with a receipt on both phones.

The problem

Paying in person still goes wrong at the counter.

“Abeg, read the account number again.”

Ten digits read aloud in a noisy market. One wrong digit and the money goes to a stranger.

“Has the alert dropped?”

Sellers wait, refresh, and still can’t tell a real credit alert from a fake screenshot.

“Network is bad today.”

When the network drops, the sale stalls, or goes back to cash and change nobody has.

Features

What Faara does.

Pay by holding phones close

Sound and Bluetooth find the person in front of you. A QR or 6-digit code works when it’s too loud.

Pay with no network

Set aside up to ₦20,000 while online, then pay phone to phone anywhere. It settles when either phone is back online.

Speak to pay

Say “Send Tolu 5k for garri” in your language and the payment fills itself in. You still approve it.

A second look before you pay

Faara warns you about risky payments, and checks suspicious messages or fake alerts for you.

Request money with a link

Ask one person, or anyone with the link, and see the moment it’s paid.

Save in dollars

Move naira into USDC, or let Faara save a share of what you receive automatically.

Safety

Nothing moves without your approval.

Your fingerprint or PIN, every time

Faara can fill a payment in for you. Only you can send it.

Same shapes on both phones

Before money moves, both screens show the same three shapes, so you never pay the wrong phone.

We never ask for your OTP

Faara will never ask for your PIN, OTP or recovery phrase. Anyone who does isn’t Faara.
