About Faara

The way Nigerians actually pay, built by people who pay that way.

What inspired it

It started at the counter.

Around Olabisi Onabanjo University, almost every payment is a bank transfer made standing in front of someone. You ask for the account number, type it while they read it out, then wait for the network. Then they wait for the alert, and look at your screen to decide whether it’s real.

When the network drops, the sale stalls. When an alert is faked, a seller loses a day’s profit. None of this is about money being hard to move. It’s about the moment two people stand face to face and try to settle up.

Cash used to solve that moment: hand it over and it’s done. Faara makes a digital payment feel the same. Hold your phones close, see the same shapes on both screens, approve, and it’s done, even with no network.

The name

Fàárà is Yorùbá, and loosely means to draw out, or pull, wonder.

That’s what we want every payment to feel like: a small moment of “wait, that just worked”, with money moving the instant two phones meet.

Mission

Make paying the person in front of you as certain as handing over cash.

For every Nigerian, on any phone, with or without network.

Vision

Every in-person payment in Nigeria, then Africa, as easy as a hum.

Starting with students and market vendors, one campus and one market at a time.
